## Language

Use positive expressions.

bad: `This is not X, not Y - Z`
good: `This is Z`.

Skip bracketed asides.

bad: `this X is so amazing innovation in Y (excitement)`
good: `Innovation X in Y makes Z significantly better`

Use quantities.

bad: `there are too much X for Y`
good: `For Y you need Z but given X`

State the amounts on both sides of a limit.

bad: `You have only 10USD left - it is impossible to do X`
good: `X costs YUSD, given 10USD`

Keep one sentence on one mechanism.

bad: `X, Y, Z - not by A, not by B.. - by ... F`
good: `By B using ... incorporating F we achive|get|obtain X with Y and Z.`

Drop words that carry no fact.

bad: `X, Y, Z, before they A.`
good: `X, Y.`

A sentence that names an absence and stops is empty. Leave it out.

bad: `What we are not proposing: X`

Use the shortest wording that still carries the fact.

bad: `One venue uplink, one org API key, 20 concurrent agent sessions`
good: `20 concurrent agent sessions using same network and API key`

bad: `With 20 tables and 6 facilitators, a director flying blind discovers stuck tables at the debrief, which is too late.`
good: `director should be able to monitor 20 tables and 6 facilitators using ....`

The file name already holds the title. The body holds the content.

bad: `AI Theater — the whole program, in one table`
good: `AI Theater`

Put totals in the lead and the breakdown under them.

bad: `120 participants · one computer each · 240 minutes · 25 tasks · 9 gates at 80% green = 96 of 120. Computer setup runs before the event, outside the budget.`
good: `4 hours program, 120 participants, 25 tasks`

Add a section when the text is unclear without it.

bad: `Section Reading the X`
good: a heading appears when the paragraph under it is unclear without that heading.

Name the objects a level is responsible for.

bad: `Level 2 walks all 120 machines: the agent is available, the dataset is in place, the videos are on disk.`
good: `Level 2 is responsible for agent availability, datasets in place, videos on disk, and handouts at all 120 seats.`

A schedule row states who runs the block and what the block contains.

bad: `Who runs the day; a map of 5 blocks, 3 breaks, and 25 tasks; an 80% threshold; how to call for help; a simulated company; voice input with the Whisper model on each machine. 9 slides`
good: `Who runs the day; a description of the 5 blocks; how to call for help and what to do when the pace falls short of the 80% threshold, with a chance to catch up with support; the simulated environment (company, messenger, mail, and the rest); voice input with the Whisper model on each machine`

A heading names the thing.

bad: `Source material (checked, with sources)`
good: `Cases`

State the structure in place order.

bad: `The five-level pyramid is read from the top: purpose sets everything else`
good: `The pyramid has five levels. The domain sits above. Work with the hardware sits below.`

Name the object of every verb.

bad: `Reversibility: switching the harness is available for 6 of 7 tools in part 5, and editing the regulation takes a day`
good: `Reversibility: switching the harness is available for 6 of 7 tools in part 5, and editing the rules file takes a day`

A sentence that reports a label and stops is empty. State the two items.

bad: `Benjamin Graham described two ways to handle money and called both of them existing.`
good: `Benjamin Graham described two ways to handle money: 1) 2)`

Put the operation and its result in one clause.

bad: `Investing is an operation that, after analysis, promises safety of principal and an acceptable return.`
good: `Investing is an operation that secures safety of principal and an acceptable return.`

Name the metrics and the shared cause.

bad: `Two numbers measure different things: the first is hardware load, the second is the financial result of the pilots.`
good: `The 2 metrics shown reflect hardware load and the financial result of the pilots, with one shared factor behind the bad outcome: the decision to start was made without a measurement before the purchase.`

Keep the term. A calque hides it.

bad: `Frame: two ways to handle money`
good: `Two frameworks for working with funds`

Describe the result. Address the reader in zero sentences.

bad: `After the talk, you roll the direction you need into your company's playbook`
good: `The talk makes it possible to build a playbook for the chosen directions`

A short sentence still names its subject and the mechanism.

bad: `Hence the second author.`
good: `Graham's margin-of-safety calculation needs modern approaches added to it. Nassim Taleb's approach fits for black swans and antifragility: designing the system's response to a model-generation change, a vendor refusal, attacks on the perimeter, and many others.`

One result, then the difference in method.

bad: a paragraph that restates both definitions before the test.
good: `Investing and speculation serve one result: the return and growth of invested resources. The difference is the method. In the first case the return comes from the work of the asset. In the second it comes from revaluing the asset.`

The title is the claim. Counts and sources sit in the body.

bad: `Benjamin Graham's and Nassim Taleb's principles applied to investments in artificial intelligence. Six vectors, entry and exit thresholds, a source for each number.`
good: `Principles of investing in artificial intelligence.`

Texts use verifiable facts and metrics. A diagram replaces a paragraph when the diagram carries the same facts.
